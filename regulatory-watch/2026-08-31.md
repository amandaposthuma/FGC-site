## Cyprus

### ℹ️ FYI — Cyprus tax reform effective January 1, 2026 — CIT raised to 15%, SDC cut to 5%, deemed dividend distribution abolished
**What changed:** Cyprus's comprehensive tax reform raised corporate income tax from 12.5% to 15% to align with Pillar Two. The Special Defence Contribution on dividends was reduced from 17% to 5% for actual distributions, and the deemed dividend distribution regime for post-2026 profits is abolished. **What it means for clients:** HNW clients using Cyprus holding companies or operating structures must update their tax models to reflect the higher 15% CIT rate, but the abolition of DDD and reduction of dividend SDC to 5% materially improves the effective tax rate on profit distribution — this may prompt a review of dividend policy and repatriation strategies for Cyprus structures. The enhanced Tax Commissioner audit powers warrant a review of historical transaction documentation.
