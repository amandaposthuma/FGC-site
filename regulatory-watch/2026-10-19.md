## USA

### 👁 WATCH — FinCEN Final Rule Permanently Exempts US Companies from BOI Reporting
**What changed:** FinCEN's August 11, 2026 final rule makes permanent the 2025 interim rule: all entities created in the United States are permanently exempt from filing beneficial ownership information reports. Only foreign entities registered to do business in a US state or tribal jurisdiction remain subject to reporting, and only for their non-US-person beneficial owners. The 2016 CDD Rule for financial institutions remains in effect. Ongoing CTA constitutionality litigation continues and should be monitored.
**What it means for clients:** US-formed entities (LLCs, corporations) are now permanently exempt from FinCEN BOI reporting — relieving significant compliance burden for domestic structures. However, foreign-formed entities registered to do business in any US state (e.g., foreign holding companies with US registered agents) must still file BOI reports disclosing their non-US beneficial owners.
**Source:** FinCEN Official Press Release — 2026-08-11 — https://www.fincen.gov/news/news-releases/fincen-removes-beneficial-ownership-reporting-requirements-us-companies-and-us

## Cyprus

### 👁 WATCH — Cyprus 2026 Tax Reform: 15% Corporate Rate, 8% Crypto Gains Tax, SDC Changes
**What changed:** Cyprus's 2026 tax reform, in effect since January 1, 2026, raises the corporate income tax rate from 12.5% to 15%, extends tax loss carry-forward to seven years, taxes crypto disposal gains at 8%, and changes dividend treatment for fund redemptions (now potentially subject to Special Defence Contribution). The 60-day residency rule was amended by removing the requirement that an individual must not be tax resident elsewhere. Pre-2026 profits remain subject to 17% SDC through 2031.
**What it means for clients:** Clients with Cyprus companies, fund structures, or Cyprus tax residency must recalibrate their tax planning: the higher corporate rate (15%) affects holding structures, crypto portfolio disposals are now taxed at 8%, and fund redemption dividends may attract SDC. The amended 60-day residency rule may benefit HNW individuals seeking Cyprus tax residency without severing other residencies.
**Source:** Deloitte Cyprus – Tax Reform and Private Wealth — 2026-01-01 — https://www.deloitte.com/cy/en/about/recognition/news/cyprus-tax-reform-and-private-wealth-considerations-for-cyprus-domiciled-investors.html
